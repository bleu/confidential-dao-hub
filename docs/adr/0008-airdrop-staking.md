# Airdrop and staking exploration

Priority: **backlog**. App status: **Soon**. Keep Airdrop / Staking as the requested roadmap entry while its scope is explored. Grouping it in navigation does not imply one shared contract or one financial mechanism.

Before implementation, decide whether airdrops and staking become separate features. Specify distribution eligibility and replay protection for airdrops; define deposits, withdrawals, reward accounting, and lockups for staking. For each, define confidentiality of participants, entitlements, balances, and rewards, authorized readers, and public disclosure. No airdrop or staking contract is implemented yet.
