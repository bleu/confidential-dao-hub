# Confidential Ops Hub

A hub for DAO treasury teams to manage confidential financial operations.

## Language

**DAO**:
The organization whose treasury operations the hub serves.

**Buyback**:
A DAO's purchase of its own token from token holders using treasury funds.

**Payroll**:
Organization-initiated payments to employees.

**Payment Request**:
A request for the DAO to pay a recipient, including invoices, contractor payments, and reimbursements.

**Reimbursement**:
A payment request to repay an expense already incurred on behalf of the DAO.

**Treasury**:
The DAO's funds and the authorized operators responsible for administering them.

## Buybacks

**Epoch**:
A buyback settlement window during which sellers submit offers against the DAO's available buyback budget.

**Offer**:
A seller's proposed token quantity and minimum acceptable price for a buyback epoch.

**Fill**:
The portion of an offer allocated against the available buyback budget, subject to the seller's price floor.

**Price Floor**:
The minimum price a seller accepts for their offered tokens.

**Disclosure**:
The publication of previously confidential information to everyone; the eligible information and timing depend on the operation.
